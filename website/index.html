What it does

Search and watch
Type a name. Movies, shows and anime come back in one list with the closest match first. Open one, press Watch, and SceneBox asks your sources (Torrentio or any other Stremio addon) for releases. Either it picks the best one for you or you choose from the list by quality, size and seeders.
Debrid
If you have a Real-Debrid account, add the key and cached releases play as a normal download at full speed. No peers involved. The key lives in the Keychain, and it only goes to community-run servers if you turn that on yourself.
Downloads
Save a movie or an episode to the device and watch it with no connection. Downloads pick up where they left off after a restart. You can cap how much space they use, restrict them to Wi-Fi, and subtitles are fetched at download time so they are there offline too.
Continue Watching
Sign in with an email and your progress, watchlist and settings follow you. Stop an episode on the iPad and the Apple TV offers to resume it. Finished episodes get a checkmark and the Watch button moves on to the next one you haven't seen.
Subtitles and audio
Subtitles come from the file itself when it has them, otherwise from OpenSubtitles, in your language by default. Audio defaults to your language when the title was made in it. A French film stays French unless you switch it.
Apple TV
Not a phone app blown up. A title's page fits on one screen: artwork behind, description and buttons on the left, episodes or cast in a row along the bottom. Move onto an episode and its full description shows up next to it.
Mac
Space to pause, arrow keys to skip, F for full screen, Esc to close. The cursor gets out of the way while you watch. Rows have arrows for scrolling with a mouse. Runs on Apple silicon and Intel.
Trailers, similar titles, cast
Title pages have a Trailer button (opens YouTube on iPhone, iPad and Mac) and a row of similar titles. Add a free TMDB key and you also get the full cast with photos, and you can tap an actor to see what else they've been in.
Links
Open a magnet: link from Safari or any other app and SceneBox plays it. scenebox://detail/movie/tt0133093 jumps straight to a title.

Questions

Where does the video come from?

From sources you set up yourself: Stremio addons like Torrentio and StremThru. Playback is peer to peer, or a direct download from your debrid provider if you have one. SceneBox doesn't host anything and has no catalogue of its own. Titles, artwork and episode lists come from Cinemeta, Kitsu and TMDB.

Is my IP visible?

With peer-to-peer streaming, yes, the same as any torrent client: the peers you connect to see your IP. Use a VPN if you care about that, or a debrid service, which fetches on your behalf. Settings says the same thing.

Where are my API keys stored?

In the Keychain. They sync to your account so your other devices get them. Community-run source servers only see them if you turn that on.

Does it work offline?

Anything you've downloaded plays offline, subtitles included. Browsing needs a connection.

Which formats play?

Pretty much anything. Playback is libVLC: MKV, MP4, AVI, HEVC, multiple audio tracks, embedded and external subtitles.

How is it installed?

Download the Mac app from this page. It's signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens like anything else you download, no warnings. The iPhone, iPad and Apple TV builds are handed out directly; email us.
